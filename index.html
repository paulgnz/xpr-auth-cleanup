🧹 XPR Auth Cleanup

Removes malicious delegated permissions (e.g. the claim permission planted by the xprdrop.com drainer) in a single transaction you sign yourself.

Read this first. This tool never sees or stores your keys — you sign in your own wallet. It only ever builds unlinkauth + deleteauth actions, shown to you in full before you sign. It cannot move funds or add permissions. Verify the domain, prefer testnet first, and if you hold staked XPR, delete the permission BEFORE unstaking (explained below).
